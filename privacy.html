Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CYPHER: AI Rap Battle & Studio ("CYPHER", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application on iOS.

1. Overview & Core Privacy Principles

We respect your privacy and creative freedom. CYPHER is designed so that you can freestyle, battle, and hone your lyrical craft without unnecessary personal data collection. Specifically:

  • We do not sell your personal information or audio recordings.
  • We do not track you across third-party websites or applications for advertising purposes.
  • We do not require you to create an account, enter your email, or provide sensitive personal identifiers to use the app.

2. Information We Collect and Process

To provide core interactive features, CYPHER collects and processes the following categories of information:

  • Audio Recordings & Microphone Data: When you record a freestyle or battle verse, your device captures your vocal audio. Microphone access is requested only when you initiate recording. Audio is temporarily processed to provide live transcription and mixed with chosen backing beats if you choose to save the battle to your local Vault.
  • Speech Recognition & Transcripts: Audio speech recognition is performed using Apple's on-device Speech Recognition Framework whenever possible. The transcribed lyrics are analyzed to generate contextual rhyming rebuttals and provide diagnostic rhythm coaching.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: StoreKit 2 processes subscription entitlements and consumable Battle Credit Packs directly through Apple. We do not store or have access to your credit card or financial billing information.
  • App Preferences & Usage Counters: On-device local storage (UserDefaults) maintains your chosen AI personas, audio preferences, and fair-use credit balances.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver advanced generative rap capabilities, the app communicates securely with trusted cloud intelligence providers via HTTPS:

  • ElevenLabs: Used for high-fidelity neural voice synthesis of AI MC personas. Text lyrics are sent to ElevenLabs to generate spoken rap audio.
  • OpenAI: Used for lyric generation, rhyme analysis, and diagnostic coaching. Text transcripts are sent to generate rap rebuttals and line improvements.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: Manages subscription validation and consumable transactions securely.

None of our third-party AI providers use your private battle verses or voice samples to train foundational public models without consent.

4. Data Storage and Retention

All saved battle recordings, transcripts, and custom Battle Cards are stored locally on your device in the app's sandboxed Documents directory. You have complete control to view, export, or permanently delete any recording at any time from within the Vault tab.

5. Children's Privacy

CYPHER is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.

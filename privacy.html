Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how JEC Labs LLC ("CYPHER", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in Cypher: Rap Battle Arena for iOS.

1. Overview

CYPHER lets you record, battle, and practice rap. You do not need to create a CYPHER account. We do not sell personal information, use an advertising identifier, or track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising. Some features process information through Apple and our cloud service providers, as described below.

Optional Cypher Accounts

You can use CYPHER without signing in. If you choose to create an account, we store your email address, an account identifier, account creation/update timestamps, a salted password hash, and sign-in sessions in Cloudflare D1. We do not store your password in plaintext. The app stores its session securely in Apple's Keychain on your device. Accounts are used for optional sign-in; they do not synchronize recordings, challenges, credits, or Apple subscriptions. We do not add account emails or identifiers to usage analytics or AI prompts.

Log Out in Settings revokes the current sign-in session. Delete Account at the bottom of Settings, confirmed with your password, removes your account, password record, and all sessions from the active account database. Expired sessions are cleaned up daily. Cloudflare may retain recoverable database backups under its platform retention policy; deleted accounts cannot sign in. Saved raps and challenges remain on your device and can be deleted separately in the app. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription; use Apple's subscription settings to cancel it.

2. Recordings, Speech, and AI Features

  • Microphone and speech recognition: When you record, the app captures your voice for playback and transcription. CYPHER uses Apple's Speech framework. Depending on the device and service availability, Apple may process audio on your device or on its servers; CYPHER does not require all recognition to happen on-device. You control microphone and speech permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Lyrics and gameplay: AI battles, judging, coaching, and lyric generation send prompts, transcribed verses, relevant battle context, and player names you provide through our Cloudflare gateway to OpenAI. Avoid including private information you do not want these services to process.
  • Generated voices: AI-generated text is sent through our gateway to ElevenLabs or OpenAI to produce spoken audio. We do not send your microphone recordings to these voice-generation services.
  • Saved content: Recordings, lyrics, battle results, preferences, and credit balances are stored locally by the app. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings. You can delete saved recordings in the Vault or remove the app's local data by deleting the app.
  • Sharing: If you choose to share recordings, battle cards, or challenge files or links, the content you select is provided to the people or services you choose. Challenge files and legacy links can contain player names and lyrics. Recipients and external services control their copies.

3. Purchases and Service Security

Apple processes subscriptions and credit-pack purchases through StoreKit. The app uses purchase and entitlement information to provide purchased features. We do not receive your card number or bank details.

Requests to our Cloudflare gateway include an Apple-signed app receipt to verify that they come from a valid app installation. The gateway processes receipt identifiers and uses a hashed identifier for usage limits and abuse prevention. The receipt itself, lyrics, and audio are not stored in our gateway's D1 database. Cloudflare processes network information, including IP addresses and operational logs, to host and protect the service.

AI usage-limit counters expire after seven days and are removed by daily cleanup, normally within eight days. Separate analytics usage-limit counters expire after two days and are removed by daily cleanup, normally within three days.

4. Optional Usage Analytics

Analytics is off until you choose to share it. Declining does not prevent you from using CYPHER. When enabled, analytics records screens and features used, activity steps, completion, cancellation, interruption and failure, subscription or credit-pack selections and purchase outcomes, and AI response times and error categories. Events include timestamps, app version/build, random identifiers for the current app session and activity, and whether the request is from Apple's test or production environment.

Analytics events do not contain your lyrics, prompts, audio recordings, player names, email address, purchase transaction IDs, or payment details. A new analytics session identifier is created when the app process launches; we do not create a persistent analytics profile identifying you across launches. Requests are authenticated with the app receipt, so our privacy disclosures conservatively treat authenticated usage and diagnostic data as linked to an installation even though the stored event table omits receipt-derived identifiers.

Cloudflare stores analytics on our behalf. Events expire after 30 days and are removed by daily cleanup, normally within 31 days. We may retain aggregate reports without session or activity identifiers to understand trends. Up to 200 unsent events may remain on your device for up to 24 hours while delivery is retried.

Your choice: Open CYPHER → Settings → Share Usage Analytics to change this setting. Turning it off stops new collection and clears unsent events on your device. It does not recall events already sent; those expire under the schedule above. The analytics choice does not disable the processing needed for AI features you choose to use.

5. Service Providers and Retention

We use HTTPS when communicating with our gateway and providers. Provider processing and retention are governed by their applicable terms and privacy policies:

  • Apple: App distribution, purchases, and speech recognition. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: Gateway hosting, optional account storage and authentication, receipt verification, abuse prevention, and optional analytics storage. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. The database retention periods above do not describe Cloudflare's separate operational logs.
  • OpenAI: Text generation, judging, coaching, and some generated speech. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default unless the customer opts in. Standard abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days, subject to its policies and exceptions. See OpenAI's API data controls.
  • ElevenLabs: Generated speech. ElevenLabs may retain submitted text and generated audio under its policies. We do not promise zero retention or that all provider model-improvement uses are disabled. See ElevenLabs' privacy policy.

Deleting a local recording does not delete copies previously shared with other people or already processed by a service provider. We retain information when reasonably necessary to respond to support requests, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service.

6. Support and External Links

If you contact us by email, we receive the address, message, and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. External store links open services governed by their own privacy policies. CYPHER's optional analytics does not collect your activity on those sites.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You can change analytics in the app, manage microphone and speech permissions in iOS Settings, delete local recordings in the Vault, and manage subscriptions through Apple. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold about you. Because stored analytics does not contain your name or contact information, we may be unable to identify particular events as yours.

8. Children's Privacy

CYPHER is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate.

9. Policy Updates and Contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. Revisions appear here with an updated date.

🎤 CYPHER APP

Cypher: Rap Battle Arena

Step into the cypher. Battle intelligent AI personas in real-time voice showdowns, pass the mic to friends, and master your freestyle flow.

Features & Experience

🏟️ The Arena

1-on-1 human versus AI voice rap battles. Spit bars live into the microphone and receive immediate, context-aware vocal rebuttals over authentic hip-hop beats.

👥 2-Player Friend Battles

Pass & Play turn-based rap battles on a single iPhone, or send round-by-round audio challenges to friends via iMessage, AirDrop, and share links.

🤖 Silicon Cypher

Autonomous AI vs AI exhibition battles. Select your favorite rap personas and watch them trade aggressive punchlines while the crowd momentum meter reacts.

🎙️ Command The Mic

On-demand solo freestyles tailored to custom keywords or random prompts, powered by high-speed neural voice synthesis.

⚖️ The Rap Judge

Get precise feedback on rhyme density, cadence, punchlines, and delivery with actionable line-by-line coaching from the AI Bar Doctor.

🏆 The Audio Vault

Save full multitrack battle recordings mixed with backing beats, and generate custom high-resolution Battle Cards ready for Instagram and TikTok.
